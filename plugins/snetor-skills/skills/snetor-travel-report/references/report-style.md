# Snetor report style — anonymised excerpts

Three excerpts adapted from real reports to calibrate **tone, language and level of detail**. To
imitate for the final rendering, not to copy word for word. They keep **the language their author
wrote them in** (two in French, one in English) and the shape of a real Snetor report, but every
person, company, country and figure is **illustrative**: none of them points to a real client.
Imitate their tone and structure, not their language: the final report is always in English.
Observe: status and sector lead each client, volumes are precise when known, the **action plan
(PA / plan of action)** is concrete, and the narrative richness (market intel, relationship,
competition) is **preserved** — not flattened into empty boxes.

---

## Excerpt 1 — Chemicals, FR, narrative (sales rep A — a mature market)

> **Commentaire général :** Le pays est un marché mature en chemicals pour SNETOR, nous
> travaillons déjà avec la quasi-totalité des acteurs. Malgré cela, de belles opportunités
> s'offrent à nous. […]
>
> **3) CLIENT C**
> Secteur : Peinture — Statut : Client actif.
> Même groupe qu'un autre de nos clients. Rencontre avec la responsable achats.
> Production annuelle : 50 000T de peinture acrylique et alkyde.
> Ils ont validé notre TiO2 et première commande passée.
> Besoins :
> - Dioxyde de Titane : 80T/mois
> - Toluène : 120KL/mois
> - White Spirit : 15KL/mois
> - Carbonate de Calcium : 10 à 15 TC/mois mais direct avec les fabricants
>
> **PA :** Envoyer échantillons de solvants et animer la relation avec offres spot dès que bons
> prix. Rechercher des fournisseurs de résines pour étoffer notre gamme (priorité).

Note: volumes per product with mixed units (T/month, KL/month, TC/month), current supplier
mentioned when known, action-oriented PA. The narrative (group, contact, context) is kept.

## Excerpt 2 — Polymers, FR, semi-structured (sales rep B — an emerging market)

> **Infos en bref :**
> - La relative stabilité récente de la monnaie est perçue comme un signe positif.
> - Les clients ressentent l'arrivée massive de petits acteurs chinois, particulièrement en PVC et PU.
>
> **CLIENT D** – PP, PET, PE, PS – 6000 tonnes (dont 2000t PET).
> Toujours aussi imposant par ses volumes. Toujours en train d'investir.
> - HDPE film : producteur X approuvé !
> - PP Thermo approuvés : producteurs Y et Z.
> - CAPS : producteur W non approuvé. Impossible de rivaliser avec les prix du producteur V.
> - Objectif de l'année : lui vendre plus de plastique en dehors du PET et PS.

Note: total volume up front, breakdown per application/grade, approved vs non-approved grades,
competition named, commercial objective. Very dense, very concrete.

## Excerpt 3 — Mixed/Polymers, EN, structured (sales rep C — an island market)

> **Client:** CLIENT E.
> **Contact:** the owner.
> **Consumption:**
> 40 MT HDPE Inj MI 4 per month.
> 20 MT HDPE Inj MI 8 UV per month.
> 20 MT PVC Compound Green per month.
> **Relationship with SNETOR:** Customer for several years, significant volumes already sold. One
> of the biggest plastic producers in the country, very good relationship with the owner.
> **Competition:** two regional distributors.
> **Developments:** Nowadays, they are producing hoses with green PVC compound.
> **Plan of Action:** We got samples of the PVC compound; the goal is to get a supplier with
> competitive offers as the quantity per month is attractive.

Note: EN because the sales rep writes in English, very regular record (Consumption /
Relationship / Competition / Developments / Plan of Action), grades with MI, relationship
history and payment behaviour kept. A single report mixes polymers and chemicals (LABSA,
caustic soda) depending on the clients.

## What to retain for the final write-up

- **Language**: the excerpts keep their author's language; the final report is in English.
- **Status + sector** at the top of each client.
- **Precise volumes** when known, otherwise "—". Keep the units as dictated.
- **Concrete action plan**: what to do, for whom, when.
- **Preserve the narrative**: market intel, relationship, competition, plant projects, rumours.
  That is what makes Snetor reports valuable — do not flatten it.
- The rendering can be **narrative** (excerpt 1) or **regular records** (excerpt 3) depending on
  the sales rep's style — match their own.
- In the final report, real names stay real: the anonymisation above only protects this reference
  file.
